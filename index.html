SME Contract Predictor

MSc dissertation, with Profitable Tender · Jan – Jun 2026

Predicting UK SME contract-award outcomes using machine learning. Merged and reconciled Contracts Finder and Find a Tender data into 417,848 modelling records, evidencing a zero-overlap structural union across identifier namespaces. Trained and compared five model architectures — Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and TensorFlow/PyTorch neural networks, achieving individual AUC-ROC of 0.753–0.812 — and deployed a weighted ensemble reaching 0.812, validated with DeLong significance tests (Z = 15.32, p < 0.001). Validated against 199 real Companies House SMEs: 89.9% fell below a 50% predicted award probability, evidence consistent with rational non-participation given structural market conditions rather than a capability gap. Used SHAP to independently verify feature importance, then shipped it as a live, public Streamlit decision-support tool with confidence intervals, feature-attribution displays, and an NLP-based tender-language complexity check.

Melbourne Housing Price Prediction

MSc coursework

Cleaned and engineered a 34,857-record Melbourne housing dataset down to 27,187 valid, model-ready records, explicitly discarding a price-per-room feature flagged for target leakage. Compared a Random Forest regressor against a deep neural network (Dense 64-32-16-1 with dropout and LR scheduling) on identical splits, iterating through three architectures. Random Forest substantially outperformed the DNN (R² 0.798 vs 0.605) — reported honestly rather than favouring the more complex model — and was deployed as a live, public Docker-based app on Hugging Face Spaces.
